# Life Path Simulation 🧭

An interactive educational web app that simulates a life journey — from
childhood through education and economics — showing how the decisions you
make shape your future. Pick at every fork, watch the 20-year projection
unfold, and learn how choices compound.

## Features

- 🧭 **Life Path Simulation** - Predict how education, savings, career and
  housing choices reshape your future
- 🎯 **10 Key Decisions** - Education (school, degree, study, graduate, learn)
  and Economics (savings, investing, career, housing, income)
- 🔮 **20-Year Projection** - See the long-term compound effect of each choice
- 📊 **Life Score** - Composite 0-100 score across 5 dimensions
- 🛡️ **Risk & Probability** - Weighted success/failure outcomes per choice
- ✏️ **Jump Back & Correct** - Click a completed progress dot or a summary row
  to revisit that decision; later choices are honestly re-asked
- 🔄 **Re-roll Partial Outcomes** - Retry a failed roll, then continue
- 📝 **Correction Tracking** - Corrections counter and ↻ badges show exactly
  where you changed your path
- 🕊️ **Free Will & Social Pressure** - Every scenario has a "common path";
  results show how many choices you made freely (your own way) vs. with the
  crowd, per phase and overall (observational — never changes your odds)
- 🪪 **Named or Anonymous Runs** - Enter your name or get a generated
  anonymous one; results are labeled with it and each run is saved as local
  statistics (on this device only)
- ⭐ **Confetti Rewards** - Celebrations at every milestone
- 🎨 **Animated Feedback** - Vibrant results and verdicts
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** - Play on any device

## How to Use

1. **Start** - Enter your name (or tap for an anonymous name) and click "Begin Your Journey" to meet your simulated self
2. **Make Choices** - Answer each education and economics scenario
3. **Watch It Unfold** - See progress dots and the 20-year projection fill
4. **Correct Anytime** - Click a green progress dot (or a row in the final
   summary) to revisit a decision — change the pick or re-roll a partial
   outcome, and later choices are re-asked
5. **Read Your Results** - Free Will & Social Pressure shows how many choices
   you made freely vs. with the crowd; Your Statistics tracks your runs on
   this device; the verdict adds recommendations
6. **New Game** - Run another simulation and compare outcomes
